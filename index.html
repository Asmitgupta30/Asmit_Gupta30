01 Profile & Voice

Geology, stars, and unreviewed footwork.

I'm Asmit Gupta, a geology major who got curious about what stars have to say about rocks.

I'm in my final year of the BS-MS at IISER Kolkata and currently writing my MS thesis at NISER, which is a polite way of saying I spend most of my days asking whether a star's chemistry can tell you what a planet is made of.

On the other end of the day, you'll mostly find me cooking in my room or on the basketball court. Occasionally you may also catch me dancing in front of a mirror, which I consider fieldwork on rhythm: it's the one place where nobody can peer-review my footwork.
